# Palette-only colors: two token layers, no color alpha

Status: accepted

Opacity modifiers (`ring-ring/50`, `oklch(1 0 0 / 10%)`) manufacture colors that belong to no agreed palette. The card spec grilling (issue #9) turned this into a system rule.

## Decision

- **Two token layers.** A palette layer holds the agreed color steps. A functional layer (`--border`, `--input`, `--ring`, `--success`, ...) aliases palette steps. Functional names are encouraged; off-palette values are not.
- **The palette is Tailwind's OKLCH palette, referenced by step name.** Functional tokens alias Tailwind v4's variables (`--primary: var(--color-orange-600)`), never literal color values, so a step name is real code and cannot drift. The theme overrides the ramps that carry an Aura swatch — `neutral`, `indigo`, `red`, `green`, `amber`, `sky`, `pink` — so each swatch pins one step. It also carries `orange`, `violet`, `teal`, and `lime` at Tailwind's stock values, so a consumer that installs the theme holds every ramp the brand, the charts, and hottrip's Stay colours use (#214). Every other ramp keeps Tailwind's stock values. Verified: Tailwind v4 emits every default-theme variable that author CSS references with `var()`.
- **Five functional ramps.** Grays come from `neutral` (chroma 0), the brand ramp is `orange` (`--primary` and `--indicator` orange-600, `--primary-text` orange-700), destructive/error is `red`, success is `green`, warning is `amber`.
- **Six group colors** — `sky`, `pink`, `teal`, `fuchsia`, `cyan`, `blue` — tell items in a set apart: a Trip's Stays, chart series, avatar fallbacks. Each hue has six aliases: `--group-<hue>` is the solid fill, with `-foreground` for text on it, `-soft` a background tint with `-soft-foreground`, and `-border` with `-border-hover`. Components and consumers use the aliases, never the ramp steps behind them. The set leaves out every hue near a reserved one — red and rose (error), orange (the brand), amber and yellow (warning), green, emerald and lime (success) — so a group never reads as a status or an action.
- **Every color operation picks a palette step.** Hover and press shades step the ramp (border-200 to border-300), never alpha, never `color-mix`.
- **Color alpha is banned repo-wide**, docs chrome included: no Tailwind `/N` color modifiers, no alpha channels in token values.
- **Element opacity stays allowed.** `disabled:opacity-50`, motion fades, and the pulse animations in ADR 0001 fade whole elements; they do not define colors.

## Consequences

- The focus ring takes `--ring`: neutral-950. It clears 3:1 against every surface by a wide margin — weakest is 13.46:1 on orange-200. A variant may ring in its own hue instead. The 2px offset sets any ring against the page background, so that is where its contrast is measured.
- The field family and the offset-ring form controls ring in `--indicator`, orange-600: input, textarea, select, combobox and its chips, date-picker, and number-field on their boundary ring, and checkbox, radio-group, and switch on their offset ring. It clears 3:1 at 3.38:1 on the orange-50 page and 3.59:1 on a white card. Their invalid state rings in `--destructive`.
- Those correction comments also restated the ring as 3px. The width was never this ADR's to set, and 3px holds only for the offset rings in #3, #6, and #7. The field ring in #4 and #5 ships 2px with no offset and is correct as shipped (#33). ADR 0003 owns the two widths.
- The status trio ships as fill pairs: `--success` green-100/green-800 (6.23:1), `--warning` amber-400/amber-900 (5.17:1), `--error` red-100/red-700 (5.36:1). Warning is a solid fill, not a tint. Error does not reuse `--destructive` (correction on #8) — a tint cannot be derived from a solid without alpha.
- Button's outline, secondary, and ghost variants ring in `--accent`, orange-200, the same step their hover washes to, on both the focus and press rings. An unpressed toggle-group chip rings in `--accent` too, the step its hover border turns. The focus ring is 1.28:1 against the orange-50 page its offset sits on and 1.36:1 against a white card, under the 3:1 indicator floor; Brian accepted that so the ring reads as the variant's own hover colour.
- The group colors (#212) alias these steps: solid 400, foreground neutral-950, soft 100, soft foreground 800, border 300, border hover 400. Text clears WCAG AA on every fill — weakest pink 6.64:1 on the solid, sky 6.45:1 on the soft. Borders separate surfaces that already differ in background and carry no floor of their own (ADR 0003); hover feedback has none either. Sky and pink use their Aura ramps; teal, fuchsia, cyan and blue have no Aura swatch and keep stock values, which already clear those floors.
- Only four of the six hues are clearly apart: cyan sits 10° from sky and fuchsia 6° from pink. A seventh hue would have to sit next to a status or the brand, so the set stays at six and relies on order. The recommended order for consumers that assign by index is sky, pink, teal, fuchsia, cyan, blue: the Aura hues lead, each lookalike pair sits three apart, and the wrap from blue back to sky spans 33°. A group color never works alone; the item always carries a text label, which also covers color-blind users.
- `--chart-1..5` are orange-500, sky-300, violet-300, teal-300, and pink-300 (#214): the brand leads, then four pale hues. Against a white card they measure 2.89, 1.47, 1.86, 1.47, and 1.98:1, all under the 3:1 non-text floor; Brian accepted that (#214), so a series is told apart by its label and position, not by its edge against the card. Neutral-950 text on slots 2–5 clears 9.20:1 or more. A chart that needs a status color takes `--success` directly.
- Avatar's `color` takes the six group colors by hue name. With no `color`, the fallback is neutral: neutral-200, with `--foreground` initials.
- Off-palette values snap to the nearest step, never keep exceptions.
- Orange has three jobs and three aliases (#214). `--primary`, orange-600, is a filled surface under text: the default button, the default badge, the pressed toggle-group chip. `--primary-foreground` is white, 3.59:1 on it, under AA for body-size text; Brian accepted that because neutral-950 on an orange fill reads muddy (6.31:1 on orange-500 by WCAG 2, but APCA Lc 49 against white's Lc 68 on orange-600). `--indicator`, orange-600, is every orange mark on a surface. `--primary-text`, orange-700, is orange as text: 4.93:1 on the page, 5.23:1 on white, 4.56:1 on `--muted`, clearing AA. Its consumers are the date-picker focused segment and the notice subject on hover.
- State indicators take `--indicator` / `--indicator-foreground` (#136): orange-600 with white, 3.59:1 on the fill. As a glyph — the checkbox tick and dash, the radio dot, the switch thumb, the today dash on a selected day — white clears the 3:1 non-text floor. As text, the calendar's selected numeral, it is under AA; Brian accepted that for the same reason as `--primary-foreground`, so every orange fill carries white. As a mark, orange-600 clears the 3:1 non-text floor at 3.59:1 on white (`--card`, `--popover`), 3.38:1 on orange-50 (`--background`), and 3.13:1 on orange-100 (`--muted`, `--secondary`). Consumers: checkbox and radio checked fills, hover borders, and marks; the switch on track and thumb; the tabs, pagination, and sidebar active bars; the sidebar current-page icon; the calendar selected fill, its text, and the today dash; the accordion hover and focus divider and chevron; the combobox and select checks; the alert info icon; the focus and press rings of the default button and the pressed toggle-group chip, which ring in their own orange-600 fill colour; the focus ring of every field-family box, the combobox chip, checkbox, radio-group, and switch; the card hover border and press ring; the avatar-group focus ring; the text-link underline at rest, which steps to orange-700 on hover and press; the progress and stepper fill; the rules an interactive table row turns on focus, and the header rule they match.
- Against orange-200 (`--accent`, `--progress-track`) orange-600 measures 2.65:1. Brian accepted that for the progress and stepper fill against its track (#214). The same step sits on orange-200 where an indicator mark lands on a highlighted surface: a select or combobox check on the highlighted row, the sidebar current-page icon on a hovered row, the calendar today dash on a hovered day or inside the range band (2.11:1 on the band's hover step), and the table focus rule beside its `--accent` row. Brian accepted those too (#214): they appear only while a row is highlighted or hovered, the same marks clear 3.38:1 or more at rest, and 2.65:1 matches the progress track. A border that only echoes a boundary already carried by semantics (the table header divider) is decorative under ADR 0003 and has no floor of its own; it takes `--indicator` only to match the focus rules in the same component.
- Hover on an orange fill steps one step darker, to orange-700, where white holds 5.23:1: a `--primary` fill (the default button and its field-icon focus fill, the pressed toggle-group chip) and an `--indicator` fill (the checked checkbox and radio, the on switch, a filled calendar day).
- The slider fill is `--slider-fill`, orange-300, on the `--secondary` orange-100 well. It measures 1.48:1 against the well, under the 3:1 non-text floor; Brian accepted that by approving the slider's design in #228. The fill repeats what the thumb and the description already carry: the thumb's `--indicator` border clears 3:1 at 3.13:1 on the well, and the description names the current step in text and as the thumb's value text. Where the thumb sits on the fill its border measures 2.11:1; its white fill keeps the border's inner edge clear. The step dots, `--muted-foreground`, hold 6.48:1 on the well and 4.37:1 on the fill.
- The calendar range band is orange-200 and steps to orange-300 under the pointer (ADR 0003). The band sits at 1.28:1 on the page and 1.36:1 on a popover; orange-100 would sit at 1.08:1 on the page and disappear. Numerals hold 13.46:1 on the band and 10.72:1 on its hover step. The band shares orange-200 with `--accent`, the idle-day hover, but the band runs edge to edge and the hover is a rounded square.
- Field boxes (input, textarea, select, combobox, date-picker, number-field) fill `--card`, white, so an editable field reads as a surface on the orange-50 page; a read-only field fills `--muted`. On focus the border takes the same white, so the boundary ring meets the fill directly. Field boxes step their border from `--input` orange-200 to orange-300 on hover, and the number-field spin button steps from `--accent` to orange-300 on press: each is the next step on the ramp the surface already shows. Hover feedback has no floor (ADR 0003).
- The switch off track is `--muted-foreground`, neutral-600, and steps to neutral-700 on hover. Its `--background` thumb clears 7.01:1 on the track and 9.44:1 on the hover step.
- The page is orange-50; cards and popovers are white; `--secondary` and `--muted` are orange-100; `--accent`, `--border`, `--input`, `--skeleton`, and `--progress-track` are orange-200 (#214). Measured with the theme's own ramp values: `--foreground` 17.20:1 on the page and 18.25:1 on white; `--muted-foreground` neutral-600 7.01:1 on the page, 7.44:1 on white, 6.48:1 on `--muted`, 5.48:1 on `--accent`; `--primary-foreground` white 3.59:1 on orange-600, the accepted case above. Orange as text takes `--primary-text`, orange-700, 4.93:1 on the page. hottrip's design language quotes 6.84, 18.65, 7.35, 7.80, and 5.29:1 for the same pairs; those were measured on Tailwind's stock `neutral` and `amber`, and the theme's Aura overrides of those two ramps give the figures above. Every other text pair in this bullet clears WCAG AA.
- `src/styles.css` (`outline-ring/50`) and the docs chrome (site header translucency) drop their alpha.

## Flip condition

A returning modal dialog needs a translucent scrim by nature. Amend this ADR then with that single sanctioned exception. See also the flip condition in ADR 0003.
